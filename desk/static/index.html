How the model decides

P(YES) = Φ(d₂)

d₂ = [ln(S / K) + (μ − σ² / 2)T] / (σ√T)

This is the Black–Scholes digital terminal probability with an estimated GBM drift. It is risk-neutral when μ is the risk-free rate; it is not a calibrated real-world forecast. It does not estimate whether a price touches a level during the window.

Selected settlement rule:

Official rules for the current contract

Net edge: side probability − probability haircut − executable ask − taker fee per contract − execution buffer. By default the buffer is the 95th percentile of adverse ask changes between polls, after 10 valid intervals per side. This measures observed quote movement, not actual future slippage. YES and NO are evaluated independently; the better positive executable edge wins.

Kelly: max(0, (p − c) / (1 − c)), where p includes the probability haircut and c includes fees and slippage. The fractional Kelly budget is limited by the per-position cap, total open cost, cash, and whole contracts at the best ask.

Book repricing targets every 500 milliseconds; reference and volatility inputs refresh more slowly. With the paper bot enabled, exits use visible bid depth for take profit, stop loss, or a bid that reaches model fair value. No real Kalshi orders are sent. Data are polled over REST and can lag the exchange.
