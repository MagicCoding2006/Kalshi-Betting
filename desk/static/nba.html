Keeping games and prices up to date

The recorder and model updater run in the background and start at login. The page reads recorded quotes every five seconds. While matching markets are open, the recorder targets a ten-second polling interval; actual source and network delays can be longer.

Buy asks and sell bids are separate from model approval and remain visible for live games or missing predictions. Quantity-weighted averages and fees are shown separately. Quote ages are local receipt ages, not exchange event times. Quotes expire after 90 seconds and current forecasts after three minutes. At tipoff, the pregame forecast becomes a historical record; no old pregame edge is used in-play.

Use “Show all today” or “Show all yesterday” to clear filters. The coverage line compares the current daily feed with the board and shows how many games have fresh quotes. These local workers require an awake, connected Mac.
